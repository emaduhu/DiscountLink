# DiscountLink 1.0.35

- Enabled Google-registered users to create a password during social registration.
- Allowed Google-registered users to sign in later with email or phone number and password.
- Allowed seller accounts to also shop the marketplace, add products to cart, checkout, view buyer orders, and rate products.
- Kept users signed in across app restarts and app switching until they explicitly log out.
- Added product-added FCM notifications with product-detail navigation when tapped.
- Expanded product-added FCM notifications to every active user account with a registered push token.
- Added fixed-amount seller discounts alongside percentage discounts.
- Fixed fixed-amount discount pricing so TZS 1,000 minus TZS 200 becomes TZS 800.
- Improved phone login normalization for Tanzanian local and country-code formats.
- Added show/hide password controls on login and registration.
- Prepared updated Google Play and App Store screenshot assets, including splash and login images.
