Version 1.0.21
- Product media links are normalized to HTTPS so videos do not fail on Android cleartext restrictions.
- Product videos now start playing when their product media page is opened.
- New products now trigger a free platform FCM campaign to active app users with device tokens.
- ClickPesa actions now show progress and prevent duplicate USSD requests while waiting.
- Improved shop form spacing prevents time controls and save actions from overlapping.
- Sellers can resend shop registration payment prompts and safely remove shops.
- Admins can view and restore removed shops while preserving their payment history.
