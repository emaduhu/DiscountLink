Version 1.0.19
- Sellers can set shop opening and closing times so buyers know when checkout is available.
- Buyers receive delivery codes by SMS and FCM notification after checkout.
- Sellers can launch paid SMS or FCM product campaigns using platform-generated messages.
- Admins can configure per-message campaign pricing and view campaign revenue.
